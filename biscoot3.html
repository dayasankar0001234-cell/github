🧠 How does this work?

1. Computer Vision

The uploaded photograph is analysed to estimate biscuit geometry, shape, condition and material characteristics.

2. Material Model

Estimated density and structural properties determine how strongly the biscuit resists its own loading.

3. Tea Interaction

Temperature and immersion time are used to model moisture absorption and material softening.

4. Structural Mechanics

The biscuit is approximated as a beam-like structure subjected to gravitational loading.

5. Prediction

The model combines geometry, material properties, tea conditions and existing damage to estimate structural failure time.

The real engineering challenge:

A single ordinary photograph cannot directly reveal absolute physical dimensions or density. For a scientifically validated version, real biscuit experiments should be collected and used to calibrate the computer-vision and structural models.
