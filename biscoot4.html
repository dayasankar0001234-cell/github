🧠 How BiscuitHub works

01 — Biscuit Detection

Your uploaded image is passed through a trained image-classification model. It determines whether the image belongs to the Biscuit class or smthgelse.

02 — Geometry

The intended computer-vision system estimates biscuit shape and geometry from the image.

03 — Material Properties

Density and structural properties are used to estimate the biscuit's resistance to deformation.

04 — Tea Interaction

Tea temperature and immersion time influence moisture absorption and material softening.

05 — Structural Mechanics

The biscuit is approximated as a beam-like structure subjected to gravitational loading.

06 — Failure

The model combines these effects to produce the final estimated biscuit survival time.

Engineering note:

The current biscuit detector is a trained image classification model. Thickness and density are still prototype estimates. A future version can replace these with a dedicated computer-vision regression model trained using real biscuit data.
